# Founder Skill Suite

Eleven skills that test a business before you launch it:

## The Eleven Skills:
1. `/founder-board` — Board of Directors (Hormozi, Thiel, Jobs frameworks)
2. `/founder-competitors` — Competitor Scout mapping direct/indirect rivals
3. `/founder-consumer` — Consumer panel simulating 100 buyer personas
4. `/founder-pricing` — Pricing strategist & margin optimization
5. `/founder-offer` — Grand Slam Offer construction
6. `/founder-cfo` — Unit economics, margins, and financial viability
7. `/founder-marketing` — Acquisition channels and marketing engine
8. `/founder-brand` — Brand identity, voice, and positioning
9. `/founder-ops` — Operations, logistics, and process design
10. `/founder-launch` — Launch playbook and go-to-market sequence
11. `/founder-plan` — Business plan compiler with computed viability verdict
