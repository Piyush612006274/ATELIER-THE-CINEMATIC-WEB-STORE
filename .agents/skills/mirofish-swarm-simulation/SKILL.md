# MiroFish Swarm Intelligence & Simulation Skill

**MiroFish** is a swarm intelligence prediction engine powered by multi-agent social simulation. By extracting seed information from product specs, marketing announcements, or market conditions, it constructs a parallel digital world where thousands of intelligent agents interact freely over simulated time.

## 5-Step Simulation Protocol

### Step 1: Graph Building & Seed Extraction
- Ingest source materials (product catalogs, pricing matrices, campaign briefs, or competitor moves).
- Extract entities, relationships, constraints, and historical context into a GraphRAG knowledge base.
- Inject individual and collective memory banks into the simulation environment.

### Step 2: Environment Setup & Persona Generation
- Synthesize diverse agent personas across demographic, psychographic, and budget cohorts:
  - Avant-garde collectors, pragmatic shoppers, luxury fashion curators, trend followers, and price-sensitive critics.
- Equip each agent with independent personality profiles, risk tolerance, and decision-making logic.

### Step 3: Dual-Platform Parallel Simulation
- Run multi-round social evolution (typically 10 to 40 rounds) simulating interactions, discussions, reviews, and word-of-mouth spread.
- Support dynamic variable injection: change pricing mid-simulation, leak limited editions, introduce stock shortages, or run promotional flash drops.

### Step 4: Report Generation & Synthesis
- The **ReportAgent** analyzes the entire trajectory of the simulated world:
  - Aggregate sentiment shifts across cohorts.
  - Predicted sales velocity and drop-off thresholds.
  - Price elasticity and willingness to pay.
  - Primary objections and viral amplification triggers.

### Step 5: Deep Interaction & Agent Debriefing
- Interactively interview individual simulated agents to understand their specific purchasing hurdles.
- Query the ReportAgent for strategic recommendations and contingency playbooks.

## ATELIER V21 Applications
- **Collection Drop Validation**: Simulate consumer reaction to upcoming capsule collections and pricing before launching inventory.
- **Campaign Angle Testing**: Compare marketing angles (e.g. brutalist minimalism vs. cinematic narrative) across synthetic buyer swarms.
- **Complement to `founder-consumer`**: Scales beyond simple prompt polls into emergent, multi-round social simulations with network effects.
